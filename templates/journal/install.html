CARELOOP ON YOUR PHONE

Keep Careloop close

Add Careloop to your home screen for easy access to your child’s journal.

iPhone / iPad

Open Careloop in Safari. Tap Share, then Add to Home Screen, and confirm Add.

Android

Open Careloop in Chrome. Use the menu and choose Install app or Add to Home screen.

Stay connected

Careloop needs an internet connection to read and save entries. If a save fails, keep your editor open. Your text stays in that open page until you can retry.

Private entries are never saved for offline reading.
