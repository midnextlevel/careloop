Professional access period

Choose a fixed date and time, or leave it blank and use a number of days after each original submission. Teachers and therapists lose access when the deadline is reached. You retain access to all submitted entries. Drafts stay private to their authors.

Restore or close access to an entry

Open an entry as owner and use its Data access panel. Set a future deadline to restore access, select Hide now to close it, or clear the deadline to return to the journal-wide period. These controls do not change the entry's audience or grant earlier membership history.

Changing the access period affects existing entries immediately on the next request. An entry's explicit deadline takes precedence. Revisions do not restart the clock.
